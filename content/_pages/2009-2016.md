## Senior Engineering Manager — Payments Activation & Onboarding
**Intuit**
*Woodland Hills, CA · August 2009 – March 2016*

Intuit is a global financial technology platform that powers prosperity for people and communities, serving small/mid-sized businesses, financial institutions, banks, consumers, and accounting professionals.

<div class="highlight-box">
    <h3>Key Achievements</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Engineering Leadership:</strong> Grew a high-performing engineering team from 2 to 12 engineers.</li>
        <li><strong>End-to-end ownership</strong> for development, architecture, design, CI/CD, and quality of payment activation across QuickBooks Online, GoPayment, QB Desktop, and QB PoS.</li>
        <li><strong>Major integrations:</strong> Adyen Payments (underwriting), Chase Payments, First Data, MasterCard sanctioned list, IP Commerce, ATT, Experian, OFAC, and OpenText Case/Document/CRM management.</li>
        <li><strong>Open Pricing initiative:</strong> Led a complex cross-team program for QB by directing 6 scrum teams across all stages of software development.</li>
        <li><strong>Innovation programs:</strong> Created market-ready products called Spark Lessons; led code jam, innovation jam, and idea jam events.</li>
        <li><strong>Recognition:</strong> Won an internal award for the most innovative idea—best of RESTful web services to solve customer problems.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

## Technical Leadership
- Mentored cross-functional engineering teams in delivering scalable payment activation services.
- Championed CI/CD pipelines, code reviews, and architectural guidelines for payments-grade reliability.
- Drove technical innovation through proofs-of-concept, hackathons, and continuous evaluation of new technologies.

## Projects & Impact
<div class="highlight-box">
    <h3>Payment Activation Platform</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Designed and delivered onboarding/underwriting flows powering QuickBooks Online, GoPayment, QB Desktop, and QB PoS.</li>
        <li>Integrated with major payment processors and risk providers (Adyen, Chase, First Data, IP Commerce).</li>
        <li>Embedded compliance checks (MasterCard sanctioned list, OFAC) and identity verification (Experian, ATT) into the activation pipeline.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Open Pricing Initiative</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Led 6 scrum teams across the full SDLC for a complex pricing modernization program at QuickBooks.</li>
        <li>Coordinated engineering, product, and finance dependencies for a multi-quarter delivery.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Innovation Programs</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Launched Spark Lessons—market-ready educational products from internal innovation work.</li>
        <li>Ran code jams, innovation jams, and idea jams that fed the team's product backlog.</li>
        <li>Earned recognition for the best RESTful web services solution to a customer problem.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

## Leadership & Business Impact
- Grew the team 6x (2 → 12) while shipping continuously across QuickBooks payment surfaces.
- Established CI/CD, code review, and architectural standards adopted across the broader org.
- Built strong cross-functional relationships with product, security, risk, and partner-engineering teams.

## Technical Skills
- **Technologies:** Java, Spring, RESTful web services, microservices, payments integrations
- **Tools:** Jenkins, Git, OpenText Case/Document/CRM
- **Architecture:** Service-oriented design, integration-heavy systems, payments compliance
