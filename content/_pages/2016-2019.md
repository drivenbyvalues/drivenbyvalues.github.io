## Senior Technical Manager — Hulu Platform Team
**Hulu HQ**
*Santa Monica, CA · March 2016 – June 2019*

The team's mission was to support and advance the datastore infrastructure landscape at Hulu.

<div class="highlight-box">
    <h3>Key Achievements</h3>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Datastore-as-a-Service:</strong> Managed datastore infrastructure team providing Database-as-a-Service across MySQL, Cassandra, and MongoDB (250+ clusters), Redis caching (70TB / 2K+ clusters), and MSSQL (2k5 → 2k17).</li>
        <li><strong>Hulu Live TV launch:</strong> Core leadership of big-event planning and execution—scaled datastore services for Super Bowl, Game of Thrones Finale, Winter Olympics, and NCAA. Cassandra peaked at 1M+ queries/sec and MySQL at 100K+ requests/sec.</li>
        <li><strong>AWS Cloud migration:</strong> Led cross-functional migration with 50+ scrum teams covering cost estimates, compatibility, Terraform templating, datastore adoption, and migration planning.</li>
        <li><strong>Cross-team collaboration:</strong> Partnered across Cloud, NoC, Chaos Engineering, Security, Platform, DevOps, and Data Stores.</li>
        <li><strong>Executive storytelling:</strong> Presented datastore vision and platform strategy at broader forums and to C-level / executive audiences.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

## Program Leadership & Strategy
- Owned datastore roadmap and capacity planning for one of the fastest-growing streaming services in the US.
- Established governance and change-management processes for stateful production systems.
- Drove incident management practices that improved MTTR for datastore incidents.
- Built strong partnerships with product engineering teams to align datastore choice to product workloads.

## Technical Initiatives & Impact
<div class="highlight-box">
    <h3>Big-Event Scaling</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Cassandra: 1M+ queries/sec sustained for live-event traffic.</li>
        <li>MySQL: 100K+ requests/sec for transactional and personalization workloads.</li>
        <li>Redis: 70TB across 2K+ clusters for low-latency caching.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>AWS Cloud Migration</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Coordinated migration of datastore fleet alongside 50+ scrum teams.</li>
        <li>Standardized Terraform templates and migration playbooks for stateful services.</li>
        <li>Delivered cost-aware migration plans with SLO guardrails on every datastore tier.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Reliability & Engineering Excellence</h3>
    <ul>
        <li>Partnered with Chaos and Security teams to harden datastore behavior under failure.</li>
        <li>Drove zero-downtime upgrades and migrations for production-critical clusters.</li>
        <li>Modernized observability for the datastore fleet across MySQL, Cassandra, MongoDB, Redis, and MSSQL.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

## Business Impact & Leadership
- Enabled Hulu's flagship live-event launches (Super Bowl, GoT Finale, Winter Olympics, NCAA) without datastore-led incidents.
- Built and grew a high-performing datastore platform team with deep cross-disciplinary expertise.
- Mentored engineers and managers across the platform organization on stateful systems thinking.

## Technical Skills
- **Datastores:** MySQL, Cassandra, MongoDB, MSSQL (2k5–2k17), Redis
- **Cloud & Infra:** AWS, Terraform, container orchestration, Linux fleet management
- **Methodologies:** Agile, SRE, Chaos Engineering, Capacity Planning
- **Stakeholders:** C-level / exec presentations, cross-team program leadership
