### Featured Articles — AI-Driven GRC Features Shipped This Year

- **[The Agent Suite — A Governance Operating System](/articles/2025-agent-suite-architecture/)** — seven coordinated agents (Intake, Triage, Risk Automation, Green Zone, Reviewer, Policy, Prioritization) with a shared governance vocabulary and explicit feedback loops. The invention is the orchestration, not any single agent.
- **[Multi-Provider LLM Infrastructure](/articles/2025-llm-infrastructure/)** — one LLMAuthService routes a dozen-plus specialized agents across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and Azure OpenAI. Database-backed prompt versioning, automatic fallback, per-call cost telemetry, and PII-residency-aware routing.
- **[Knowledge Graph for Cross-Agent Context](/articles/2025-knowledge-graph/)** — every Agent Run reads from and writes to an organizational graph of departments, products, data categories, risks, controls, and precedents — institutional memory that compounds with every assessment.
- **[Intelligent Assignment Engine](/articles/2025-intelligent-assignment/)** — auto-routes assessments to the best reviewer using a transparent score that blends product, business unit, and skill fit with workload. Configurable `skill_floor` vs. `workload_weight` knobs let leadership tune the quality-vs-fairness trade-off per review type.
- **[Continuous Auto-Prioritization](/articles/2025-continuous-auto-prioritization/)** — re-scores every open assessment whenever its facts change (new evidence, scope mutation, contradicted claim, deadline shift), with hysteresis bands and cool-downs to avoid priority whiplash.
