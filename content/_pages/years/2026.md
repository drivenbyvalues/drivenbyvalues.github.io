### Featured Articles — AI-Driven GRC Features Shipped This Year

- **[Grounded Review Summaries](/articles/2026-grounded-review-summaries/)** — every sub-review summary is generated from retrieved evidence with citations on every claim. Hybrid retrieval (vector + BM25 + entity graph) feeds a Verifier agent that re-checks each citation actually entails the claim before a human ever sees it.
- **[MCP Server — Natural Language to Structured Assessments](/articles/2026-mcp-server/)** — a Model Context Protocol server that turns free-form descriptions into fully structured, JWT-authenticated assessments with field-level provenance and confidence scoring.
- **[Green Zone Agent — Reusable Governance Envelopes](/articles/2026-green-zone-agent/)** — stewards author pre-approved boundaries; the agent maps each assessment to its best-fit zone, surfaces conditions, and escalates anything that crosses a hard exclusion. Bounded autonomy for responsible AI.
- **[AI-Assisted Template Transformation](/articles/2026-template-transformation/)** — five-strategy semantic mapping engine (exact, pattern, semantic, contextual, AI-inferred) with weighted confidence aggregation, dual backend/frontend AI failover for high (99%+) availability, and adaptive learning from history.
- **[DPIA & LIA Reviews](/articles/2026-dpia-lia-reviews/)** — Data Protection Impact Assessments (GDPR Art. 35) and Legitimate Interest Assessments (Art. 6(1)(f)) authored as structured templates, pre-filled from intake and evidence with citations, and validated by counsel. Cycle time moves from weeks to days while staying regulator-ready.
