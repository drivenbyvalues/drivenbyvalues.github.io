# Mridangam — Carnatic Classical Percussion

## What It Is

The mridangam is the primary percussion instrument of Carnatic (South Indian) classical music — a double-headed, barrel-shaped drum carved from a single block of wood (traditionally jackfruit), with each head tuned differently and played with the fingers and palms of both hands to produce a wide vocabulary of tones and syllables (*sollukattu*). It sits at the rhythmic center of a Carnatic concert, in constant conversation with the lead instrument or vocalist rather than simply keeping time behind them.

## Training &amp; Achievement

I trained through the formal Carnatic percussion examination track, clearing both the **Junior** and **Senior** grade examinations in Mridangam. In the **All India examination for the Senior grade**, I secured **3rd Rank** nationally.
