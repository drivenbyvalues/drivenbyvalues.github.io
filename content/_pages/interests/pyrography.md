# Pyrography — Wood Burning Art

## What It Is

Pyrography is the art of burning designs into wood using a heated tool — in my case, a soldering iron — building up shading and line work through controlled burning rather than paint or ink. Some pieces stay pure burned linework; others add a layer of color on top once the burning is done.
